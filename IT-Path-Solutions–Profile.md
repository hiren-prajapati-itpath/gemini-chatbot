* Target Participants: Existing clients, influencers, business consultants, students.

---

#### **2\. Sales Partnership**

* Acts as a Business Development Representative to generate qualified and confirmed business sales leads directed to IT Path Solutions.

* **Role**: Stay involved at the pre-sales level and during initial discovery to facilitate tech-commercial discussions with prospects.

* **Responsibilities**: Marketing and sales efforts to attract qualified prospects, evaluating prospects, and taking deals from negotiation to close—working jointly with IT Path Solutions’ internal business consulting team.

* Target Participants: Business consultants, technology advisors, graduates/interns, digital agencies.

---

#### **3\. Development Partnership**

* Tailored to startups or mid-sized digital agencies seeking a cost-effective, all-in-one technology development partner.

* **Role**: Ideal for agencies wanting to expand their service offerings but lack in-house resources—especially in specialized technologies.

* **Benefits**: Long-term partners enjoy discounted development fees and tiered rebates based on business volume.

* Target Participants: Startups, freelancers, digital agencies, small and medium-sized businesses.
